The starting point / A place and a year

From reading history to living it.

The globe started as a history reader: pick a country and a year to read about that moment.

I wanted to make it personal. Choose a person who could have lived there, then decide what they do.

01 / Text · July

Text made the choices work.

Read a scene. Decide what happens next.

What we built
Scenes with two suggested actions, space to write your own, and memory of your choices.
What we learned
Early scenes lost track of people. We began planning the cast and key events before each scene.

02 / Visual novel · July–September

The visual novel added faces and voices.

I brought the cast, dialogue, and choices onto one stage.

What we built
Painted backgrounds, character expressions, and voiced dialogue on web and iPhone.
What we learned
Characters couldn’t reliably pick up objects. Film let us generate the people and action together.

03 / Film · September–October

Film made the whole scene move.

Each shot starts from the last frame of the one before it.

What we built
A generated film that pauses for choices. The next shots are prepared ahead of time.
What we learned
Video cost more, characters could change appearance, and the film set the pace. We tried comics to give readers control.

04 / Comic · October

Comics let readers set the pace.

Keep the pictures and choices. Let people read at their own pace.

What we built
A story told panel by panel. Choose a response, write your own, or start a story from an idea.
How it works
We add lettering separately, check the art, and prepare the next branches before the reader chooses.

Shortened recordings. Generation waits are sped up where marked in the demos.

Our team / Product, design & engineering

From model output to playable stories.

Exploring four formats helped us work out what to keep, what to change, and what to build next.

Our team
Built the story engine, memory, voices, visuals, and choices as one product.
My role
Led product and design. Played the builds, identified problems, and wrote briefs for the team and our coding agents.
My background
Previously worked on Fortnite’s social experience and cross-platform text chat at Epic Games.

More stories to build.

We want to keep building as a team. For Runway Labs, we’d bring experience taking generative media from a model output to something people can play.
